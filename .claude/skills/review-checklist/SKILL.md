# Review checklist

Work through each item. Record a finding only where something is wrong or uncertain. Severity:
**high** means the reviewer should not approve as is. **medium** means fix it or explain it. **low** means a note.

## Pack
- [ ] No blocking checks remain (`get_validation_report`).
- [ ] Every warning has an explanation somewhere: a workpaper note, the commentary or a follow-up.
- [ ] Mapping fallbacks are in a sensible line for the account's nature. A job cost in overheads is a medium finding.

## Workpapers
- [ ] Each conclusion follows from its rows; tie-out differences are nil or explained.
- [ ] Spot-check at least two references per workpaper by opening the document (`read_document`).
- [ ] Every exception (`!`) has a preparer note with a proposed action.

## Commentary
- [ ] Every material line is covered, and the summary matches net profit direction.
- [ ] The cause given is the *real* driver of the variance, not just the biggest transaction in the account. Compare against the budget phasing and other evidence in the context.
- [ ] No claim of intent, approval or agreement without an email or document saying so.
- [ ] Timing differences are called timing, so the owner is not misled about next month.
- [ ] No figure is presented with more certainty than the evidence gives.
- [ ] Readable by a non-accountant.

## Not your job
Approving, rejecting, editing the draft and judging tax treatment are not your job. Recommend; the human decides.
